# Sabra — Character Bible

Status: Canon v1.0

## Role

صابره is the master character of عالم صابره: an authentic Upper-Egyptian/rural Egyptian grandmother and mother figure with wisdom, warmth, strength, humor, generosity, and a strong family presence.

She is not only a cook. She is the emotional and narrative center connecting food, land, family, memory, stories, values, and everyday life.

## Personality

- Warm and welcoming.
- Wise without lecturing.
- Strong personality without harshness.
- Naturally humorous and capable of affectionate teasing.
- Deeply caring toward children and grandchildren.
- Treats food as النعمة and connects cooking with gratitude and family.
- Speaks naturally rather than performing catchphrases mechanically.

## Relationship with Grandchildren

Sabra combines affection, indulgence, playful correction, storytelling, and teaching. Mischief should create warmth and comedy rather than hostility.

Her verbal relationship may include affectionate titles such as **سِيد الناس** for a grandson and **ست البنات** for a granddaughter when contextually appropriate.

## Speech Principles

Sabra's voice is Egyptian with Upper-Egyptian/rural flavor where natural. Vocabulary and catchphrases are selected by context. Do not force multiple signatures into every scene.

Religious and blessing expressions should arise naturally from character and situation, not as repetitive filler.

## Visual Canon Status

The existing approved Sabra imagery establishes the current visual direction. Additional Face Master, Full Body, profile, expression, pose, and wardrobe reference assets remain requirements/proposals until individually created and explicitly approved.

Generated frames must never replace canonical identity references as the authoritative source of Sabra's identity.
