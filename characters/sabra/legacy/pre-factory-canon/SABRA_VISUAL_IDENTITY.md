## 1. Purpose

This document defines Sabra's canonical visual identity for image and video production.

Its purpose is to prevent character drift between generations, scenes, environments, wardrobe changes, camera angles, and production tools.

Sabra may change clothes, location, activity, expression, and lighting.

She must remain recognizably the same woman.

---

## 2. Identity Priority

When generating Sabra, preserve visual identity in this order:

1. Face
2. Eyes and kohl
3. Facial proportions
4. Body proportions
5. Skin tone and texture
6. Headscarf styling
7. Signature earrings
8. Hands and nails
9. Gold jewelry language
10. Wardrobe and embroidery language

Wardrobe consistency must never take priority over facial identity.

---

## 3. Face Lock

Sabra has a soft, rounded Egyptian face.

The face must remain:

- Soft
- Naturally full
- Warm
- Mature
- Feminine
- Recognizably Egyptian

Avoid:

- Sharp cheekbones
- Hollow cheeks
- Strong angular jaw
- Narrow model-like face
- Artificial facial symmetry
- Glamour-model transformation

Changing expression must not change facial structure.

Changing camera angle must not create a different woman.

---

## 4. Eyes Lock

Sabra's eyes are a signature identifier.

They must remain:

- Wide
- Dark
- Attractive
- Expressive
- Warm
- Naturally proportioned

Black kohl is required.

The kohl should enhance the natural shape and width of the eyes.

Avoid:

- Heavy smoky makeup
- Fashion eyeliner
- Excessive lashes
- Colored contact appearance
- Narrowing the eyes between generations

The eyes should retain their identity in neutral, smiling, laughing, serious, and surprised expressions.

---

## 5. Skin

Sabra has a warm Egyptian wheat/olive-brown complexion.

Preserve natural skin texture.

Allow:

- Fine expression lines
- Natural pores
- Subtle sun exposure
- Normal mature skin variation

Avoid:

- Plastic skin
- Porcelain skin
- Excessive smoothing
- Glamour retouching
- Artificially pale skin
- Major skin-tone changes caused by scene lighting

Lighting may affect appearance but must not redefine canonical skin tone.

---

## 6. Age Appearance

Sabra is a young grandmother.

She must appear mature but active and relatively young.

She became a grandmother because she married and started her family early.

Do not visually age her simply because she is a grandmother.

Avoid:

- Elderly facial structure
- Frailty
- Deep aging effects
- Gray-haired elderly styling
- Stooped posture

Likewise, do not de-age Sabra into a woman in her twenties or early thirties.

---

## 7. Body Lock

Canonical height:

**165 cm**

Sabra has a naturally full, healthy, feminine body.

Her build should communicate:

- Strength
- Health
- Maturity
- Practical physical capability
- Natural fullness

She is not thin.

Avoid:

- Fashion-model proportions
- Narrow waist exaggeration
- Fitness-model physique
- Obesity exaggeration
- Significant body-size drift between scenes

Her body proportions must remain stable across wardrobe changes.

---

## 8. Posture and Movement

Sabra moves like a woman familiar with her environment and daily work.

Her posture is:

- Confident
- Relaxed
- Grounded
- Comfortable

Movement should feel efficient rather than staged.

Avoid exaggerated theatrical body language.

When working, she should look experienced rather than as though she is demonstrating an unfamiliar task for camera.

---

## 9. Headscarf Identity

The headscarf is part of Sabra's visual silhouette.

The exact fabric, color, and pattern may change.

The styling language must remain recognizable.

The scarf should feel:

- Practical
- Natural
- Culturally coherent
- Integrated into daily life

Avoid:

- High-fashion hijab styling
- Elaborate television styling
- Costume-like wraps
- Random scarf geometry between consecutive shots

Within the same scene, scarf design and wrapping must remain continuous.

---

## 10. Signature Earrings

Traditional Sa'idi gold earrings are a signature asset.

They should remain visually recognizable across productions.

The earrings must not randomly change design between shots in the same sequence.

They should feel authentic and elegant without becoming oversized theatrical props.

---

## 11. Gold Jewelry

Gold is present but restrained.

Permitted visual language includes:

- Signature earrings
- Simple bracelet or bracelets
- Simple ring
- Modest necklace

Avoid excessive stacking or luxury-display styling.

Jewelry continuity must be maintained within the same scene.

---

## 12. Hands

Sabra's hands are a high-priority continuity area.

Hands must appear:

- Feminine
- Clean
- Healthy
- Naturally proportioned
- Consistent with Sabra's age and lifestyle

Pay particular attention to close shots.

Avoid:

- Extra fingers
- Missing fingers
- Fused fingers
- Distorted joints
- Excessively aged hands
- Model-perfect artificial hands

---

## 13. Nails

Sabra's nails are always:

- Short
- Clean
- Natural
- Even
- Carefully maintained

Avoid:

- Long nails
- Nail extensions
- Artificial shapes
- Heavy nail polish
- Decorative nail art

Beautiful cleanliness is part of Sabra's visual identity.

---

## 14. Wardrobe System

Sabra does not wear a permanent uniform.

Wardrobe varies according to context.

Canonical contexts include:

- Home
- Field and farm
- Outdoor activity
- Family gathering
- Special occasion
- Travel or outing

Her wardrobe remains culturally coherent and modest.

The primary clothing language is based around the galabeya and practical Upper Egyptian garments.

- Fashion makeup
- Glamour photography styling

- Seriousness

Sabra must remain recognizable at:

- Extreme close-up
- Close-up
- Medium close-up
- Medium shot
- Medium full shot
- Full body
- Environmental wide shot

For close shots, prioritize face, eyes, skin, hands, and jewelry continuity.

- Front
- 45-degree left
- 45-degree right
- Side
- Back
- Natural working angles

## 21. Lighting

Sabra may appear under:

- Natural morning light
- Daylight
- Outdoor shade
- Warm interior daylight
- Golden-hour light
- Practical indoor lighting

Lighting can change mood but must not change identity.

Avoid excessive cinematic lighting that makes Sabra look like a fashion or luxury commercial unless a production specifically requires that aesthetic.

Sabra may appear in multiple environments.

Examples include:

- Home
- Agricultural fields
- Farm areas
- Nile surroundings
- Indoor spaces
- Outdoor working areas
- Family environments

Environment changes must not trigger character redesign.

Sabra enters the environment.

The environment does not redefine Sabra.

## 23. Image Generation Continuity

For new image generation:

1. Start from approved Sabra references.
2. Preserve facial identity before changing wardrobe or environment.
3. Preserve body proportions.
4. Match age appearance.
5. Maintain eyes and kohl.
6. Maintain signature earrings.
7. Verify hands when visible.
8. Verify scene-specific wardrobe continuity.
9. Reject generations that create a different woman.

A visually beautiful image is not acceptable if Sabra's identity has drifted.

## 24. Video Continuity

Video production introduces additional risks.

Monitor:

- Facial morphing
- Eye-shape changes
- Body-size changes
- Jewelry appearing or disappearing
- Scarf pattern changes
- Clothing changes between frames
- Hand deformation
- Age fluctuations
- Skin-tone fluctuations
- Lip-sync distortion
- Identity changes during head turns

Video validation must prioritize character continuity over spectacle.

## 26. Rejection Criteria

Reject generated material when:

- Sabra looks like another woman
- Face shape significantly changes
- Eyes lose their established identity
- Body becomes thin
- She appears elderly
- Skin becomes artificially retouched
- Kohl becomes excessive
- Hands are visibly malformed
- Nails become long or artificial
- Jewelry becomes excessive
- Cultural styling becomes theatrical
- Wardrobe changes unexpectedly within a continuous scene
- Character identity changes during video motion

---

## 27. Canonical Visual Rule

**Change the scene, not the woman.**

Sabra may wear different clothes, stand in different places, experience different emotions, and appear under different lighting.

She remains Sabra.
