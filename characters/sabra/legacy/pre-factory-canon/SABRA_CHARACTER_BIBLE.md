## 1. Character Overview

Sabra â€” ØµØ§Ø¨Ø±Ø© â€” is the central character of **Sabra World**.

Sabra is an Egyptian woman of Sa'idi origin. She grew up in Upper Egypt, married young, and later moved with her family to a village in rural Giza, where she has lived for many years. Sabra is a mother and a young grandmother who started her family early in life.

She is not elderly. She remains active, energetic, feminine, strong, and deeply involved in the daily life of her home, land, animals, and family.

Sabra has natural authority and presence. She is respected by her family, but she is not severe or emotionally distant.

Sabra should feel like a real woman whose life existed before the camera arrived.

The camera enters **her world**. Sabra does not change herself to become a television personality.

## 3. Physical Identity

### Height

Canonical height:

**165 cm**

This value should remain consistent when Sabra appears beside furniture, children, adults, animals, counters, doors, or other scale references.

### Body

Sabra is not thin.

She has a naturally full, healthy, feminine body consistent with an active rural woman.

Her body should communicate:

- Strength
- Health
- Maturity
- Femininity
- Physical capability
- Natural fullness

Avoid fashion-model proportions or artificially sculpted fitness proportions.

### Posture

Sabra carries herself confidently.

Her posture is relaxed but grounded. She does not appear timid, fragile, or physically weak.

Her movements should look familiar and experienced when she works in environments she knows well.

---

## 4. Face

Sabra has a soft, rounded Egyptian face.

Her facial structure must remain soft.

Avoid:

- Strong cheekbone prominence
- Sharp jawlines
- Hollow cheeks
- Angular model-like facial structure

Her beauty should feel natural, familiar, warm, and distinctly Egyptian.

Natural mature skin texture is desirable.

Do not over-retouch her face or produce artificial plastic skin.

---

## 5. Eyes

Sabra's eyes are one of her most important signature features.

They are:

- Wide
- Attractive
- Warm
- Expressive
- Dark
- Highly communicative

Her eyes use elegant black kohl.

The kohl should clearly enhance the eyes while remaining tasteful and natural.

It must never become theatrical, excessively heavy, or glamorous.

Sabra should be capable of communicating authority, affection, humor, skepticism, surprise, and playful warning through her eyes alone.

---

## 6. Smile and Laugh

Sabra's smile is warm and recognizable.

Her laugh is a major character signature.

It should feel:

- Genuine
- Open
- Spontaneous
- Joyful
- Contagious

Sabra does not perform laughter for the camera.

When she laughs, it should feel as though something genuinely amused her.

Her laugh will later become an important component of her voice identity.

## 8. Jewelry

Gold is part of Sabra's visual identity, but it is restrained.

### Signature Earrings

Traditional Sa'idi gold earrings are a core signature element.

They should remain recognizable across appearances.

The earrings must feel culturally authentic rather than theatrical costume jewelry.

### Other Gold

Sabra may wear:

- Simple gold bracelets
- A simple ring
- A modest necklace

Jewelry should never overwhelm the character.

Sabra wears gold because it belongs naturally to her life and identity, not to demonstrate wealth.

## 9. Headscarf

Sabra normally wears a traditional practical headscarf.

The scarf is part of her recognizable silhouette.

Colors and patterns may vary with wardrobe and context.

However, the styling should remain consistent with Sabra's established identity.

Avoid making the scarf appear like fashion styling created specifically for television.

Sabra does not wear one permanent costume.

**Different looks. Same Sabra.**

Her wardrobe changes according to:

- Home life
- Agricultural work
- Outdoor activities
- Family gatherings
- Special occasions
- Travel or outings

Her primary garment language is based around the traditional galabeya and culturally coherent modest clothing.

Preferred palette includes:

- Black
- Olive green
- Burgundy
- Indigo and navy
- Earth brown
- Terracotta
- Warm beige
- Mustard
- Deep teal

Clothing should feel practical, authentic, modest, feminine, and lived-in.

- Timing
- Facial expressions
- Reactions
- Everyday observations
- Family interaction
- Playful responses

## 13. Family Identity

Sabra married young and became a mother early.

She is now a grandmother while still relatively young and active.

She has sons, daughters, and grandchildren at different stages of life.

Her family respects her.

Her grandchildren can reveal a softer and more playful side of her personality.

Sabra may tease them, affectionately correct them, laugh with them, listen to them, and respond naturally to their requests.

The family exists inside Sabra's world, but Sabra remains the central character.

## 14. Home and Land

Sabra's current home is located in a rural village in Giza, surrounded by green agricultural land and connected to the Nile agricultural environment.

The land is not decorative scenery.

It is part of Sabra's daily life and identity.

Sabra regularly goes to the fields and gathers fresh produce.

Her relationship with the land should feel habitual and practical rather than staged for camera.

## 16. Household Philosophy

Sabra's household depends heavily on what the land and animals provide.

She purchases relatively little food from outside compared with an urban household.

This principle is important to understanding her lifestyle and world.

It should emerge naturally from the environment and her behavior rather than being repeatedly explained to the audience.

## 17. Camera Presence

Sabra must never feel like a generic television presenter.

The desired impression is:

**Sabra is living her normal life, and the camera happens to be there.**

She is comfortable with the camera but does not constantly perform for it.

She may:

- Look directly at camera
- Speak to someone off camera
- Continue an activity while speaking
- React naturally to family members
- Laugh spontaneously
- Pause and think
- Use expressive gestures

Her behavior should remain grounded and believable.
