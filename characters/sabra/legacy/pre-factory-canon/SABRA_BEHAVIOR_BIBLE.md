# SABRA BEHAVIOR BIBLE

**Character ID:** SABRA
**Version:** 1.0
**Project:** Sabra World / عالم صابرة
**Status:** Behavioral Identity Definition
**Character Bible:** `SABRA_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how Sabra behaves on screen.

Visual identity determines what Sabra looks like.

Behavioral identity determines whether she feels like Sabra.

A technically perfect visual reproduction is not acceptable if the character moves, reacts, speaks, or behaves like a different person.

---

## 2. Behavioral Core

Sabra is:

- Confident
- Warm
- Grounded
- Experienced
- Naturally authoritative
- Affectionate
- Practical
- Quick-witted
- Light-hearted
- Emotionally expressive
- Hospitable

She does not need to prove her authority.

Her presence creates authority naturally.

---

## 3. Energy

Sabra has healthy, active energy.

She is not:

- Frail
- Slow because of age
- Hyperactive
- Nervous
- Constantly performing

Her normal pace is purposeful and relaxed.

When she knows what she is doing, her movements are efficient.

---

## 4. Physical Presence

Sabra occupies space comfortably.

She stands naturally and confidently.

She does not shrink herself physically when another person enters the scene.

Her shoulders remain relaxed.

Her posture communicates:

**This is my home. This is my land. I know what I am doing here.**

This confidence must never become arrogance.

---

## 5. Walking

Sabra walks with purpose.

Her walk should feel familiar with the terrain.

In the house, she moves comfortably without hesitation.

In the field, she navigates the land naturally.

She should not walk like:

- A fashion model
- A television host entering a set
- An elderly fragile woman
- Someone unfamiliar with rural terrain

---

## 6. Standing

Sabra's default standing pose is relaxed and grounded.

She may naturally:

- Rest one hand near her waist
- Hold an object
- Gesture while speaking
- Shift weight slightly
- Look toward someone nearby
- Turn toward an activity

Avoid repetitive artificial presenter poses.

---

## 7. Sitting

Sabra sits comfortably rather than ceremonially.

Her sitting posture depends on context.

She may sit:

- On a normal chair
- On a bench
- On a low stool
- In an outdoor family space
- Near family members

She should appear comfortable in her own environment.

---

## 8. Hands and Gestures

Sabra's hands are expressive.

Her gestures support her speech rather than replacing it.

Common gesture language may include:

- Open palm while explaining
- Small pointing gesture
- Hand toward chest during personal emphasis
- Gentle dismissive wave during teasing
- Beckoning gesture
- Calling gesture
- Hand movement expressing disbelief
- Affectionate touch toward family

Avoid exaggerated theatrical gestures.

---

## 9. Face and Eyes

Sabra communicates strongly through her eyes.

A look can communicate:

- Approval
- Warning
- Humor
- Suspicion
- Affection
- Curiosity
- Pride
- Surprise

She does not need exaggerated facial animation.

Her wide eyes and expressive eyebrows provide significant emotional range.

---

## 10. Smile

Sabra smiles naturally.

She does not maintain a permanent presenter smile.

Her smile appears because something causes it.

Sometimes the smile begins in the eyes before becoming visible across the whole face.

---

## 11. Signature Laugh

Sabra's laugh is a major behavioral signature.

It is:

- Warm
- Genuine
- Open
- Spontaneous
- Infectious

When she genuinely laughs, her body may participate naturally:

- Shoulders move slightly
- Eyes narrow naturally
- Head may tilt
- Hand may move instinctively
- She may briefly turn toward another person

Never make every humorous moment produce the same identical laugh animation.

---

## 12. Authority

Sabra has natural authority.

When she becomes serious, she does not need to shout.

Authority may appear through:

- Eye contact
- Short silence
- Change in facial expression
- Lower or firmer delivery
- Small hand gesture
- Direct wording

Her family understands her signals.

A single look may sometimes be enough.

---

## 13. Anger

Sabra may become annoyed or angry.

However, anger is not her default personality.

She should not become:

- Aggressive
- Cruel
- Constantly shouting
- Humiliating toward family
- Cartoonishly angry

Her strongest reactions should remain believable.

---

## 14. Humor

Sabra's humor comes naturally from life.

Sources include:

- Quick responses
- Observations
- Facial reactions
- Family behavior
- Timing
- Playful exaggeration
- Gentle teasing
- Unexpected comments

She does not behave like a comedian performing jokes for an audience.

Humor should emerge from character.

---

## 15. Children

Children reveal one of Sabra's warmest dimensions.

With grandchildren she may:

- Smile more easily
- Tease
- Correct them affectionately
- Listen seriously to childish requests
- Pretend to be annoyed
- Give them a knowing look
- Laugh with them
- Touch their head or shoulder affectionately
- Draw them closer
- Respond to interruptions naturally

Children are comfortable approaching her.

They respect her but are not afraid of her.

---

## 16. Playful Scolding

Sabra may use playful scolding with grandchildren.

The emotional message should remain:

**I am correcting you because you belong to me and I care about you.**

It should not feel threatening.

A look, small gesture, or short phrase may carry more weight than shouting.

---

## 17. Adult Family Members

With adult children, Sabra remains the family matriarch without treating them like small children.

She may:

- Give advice
- Disagree
- Joke
- Listen
- Ask direct questions
- Express pride
- Express concern
- Challenge a decision

The relationship should feel established by years of family history.

---

## 18. Guests

Sabra is naturally hospitable.

When welcoming someone:

- Her face opens
- Her body turns toward the guest
- She may gesture them inside or toward a seat
- She communicates that their presence is welcome

Hospitality should feel habitual rather than ceremonial.

---

## 19. Camera Relationship

The camera is allowed inside Sabra's life.

It does not control her life.

Sabra may speak directly to camera when appropriate.

But she does not constantly:

- Face camera
- Smile at camera
- Present objects to camera
- Wait for camera approval
- Perform exaggerated reactions

She may continue an activity while speaking.

She may look away.

She may respond to someone off screen.

She may briefly forget the camera is present.

---

## 20. Direct-to-Camera Moments

When Sabra addresses the audience directly, the relationship should feel personal.

She speaks as though a real person is standing nearby.

Avoid formal broadcasting behavior.

The audience should feel:

**Sabra is talking to me.**

Not:

**A presenter is addressing viewers.**

---

## 21. Listening

Sabra is an active listener.

When another person speaks she may:

- Maintain eye contact
- Continue a simple activity
- React with her eyes
- Raise an eyebrow
- Smile
- Pause
- Turn toward them
- Respond with a small sound or gesture

She should not freeze unnaturally while waiting for dialogue.

---

## 22. Thinking

When Sabra thinks, the behavior is subtle.

Possible signals:

- Brief pause
- Eyes move slightly
- Small head movement
- Lips tighten gently
- Hand pauses during an activity

Avoid exaggerated "thinking poses."

---

## 23. Working Behavior

Sabra is experienced in her environment.

When performing familiar activities, she should not constantly look at her hands to understand what she is doing.

Her body has practical memory.

Actions should appear:

- Confident
- Familiar
- Efficient
- Safe
- Natural

---

## 24. Field Behavior

The field belongs to Sabra's normal life.

She may:

- Walk between crops
- Inspect plants
- Pick produce
- Carry baskets
- Bend or crouch naturally
- Speak while working
- Look toward distant family members
- React to animals or environmental activity

She must never appear like a tourist visiting a farm.

---

## 25. Animal Interaction

Sabra is familiar with the household animals.

She should not appear nervous around animals she knows.

Interactions should communicate routine familiarity.

She may:

- Approach calmly
- Call an animal
- Touch it naturally
- Carry feed
- Observe behavior
- React to an animal doing something unexpected

Animals are part of her daily environment.

---

## 26. Environmental Awareness

Sabra knows where things belong.

She moves through her home, land, and work areas without appearing lost.

She may reach for objects naturally without visually searching for them first.

This reinforces the idea that these environments existed before filming began.

---

## 27. Emotional Range

Sabra may experience:

- Joy
- Affection
- Pride
- Concern
- Curiosity
- Surprise
- Disbelief
- Annoyance
- Determination
- Nostalgia
- Gratitude
- Playfulness

No single emotion should dominate the character.

Her authority and humor can coexist.

---

## 28. Gratitude

Gratitude is a natural part of Sabra's worldview.

Moments of gratitude should feel sincere and integrated into life.

They should not feel inserted as artificial performance.

Family moments may naturally include appreciation for:

- Family
- Land
- Harvest
- Health
- Shared meals
- Daily blessings

---

## 29. Silence

Sabra does not need constant dialogue.

Silence may be used for:

- Thinking
- Observing
- Reacting
- Emotional emphasis
- Enjoying family presence
- Working

Her character should remain alive even when she is not speaking.

---

## 30. Interaction Rhythm

Conversation should not feel mechanically alternating.

People may:

- Interrupt naturally
- Laugh before a sentence finishes
- React silently
- Continue an activity
- Speak from outside the frame
- Enter or leave a scene

Sabra's reactions should acknowledge these events naturally.

---

## 31. Behavioral Continuity

Across shots, preserve:

- Emotional state
- Energy level
- Relationship dynamics
- Activity state
- Gesture logic
- Physical orientation
- Object interaction

Do not reset Sabra emotionally every time the camera angle changes.

---

## 32. Video Generation Risks

Reject or regenerate video when Sabra:

- Moves like a different person between shots
- Becomes excessively theatrical
- Develops presenter behavior
- Moves like an elderly woman
- Becomes unnaturally energetic
- Uses repetitive AI gestures
- Stares continuously into camera
- Freezes while another person speaks
- Loses emotional continuity
- Interacts incorrectly with objects
- Shows physically impossible movements
- Changes personality because wardrobe or environment changed

---

## 33. Behavioral Rule

**Sabra never performs being Sabra. She simply is Sabra.**

Her behavior comes from a lifetime inside her world.

The audience should feel that the camera discovered her life rather than created it.
