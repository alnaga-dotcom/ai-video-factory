# SABRA NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

**Character ID:** SABRA
**Version:** 1.0
**Project:** Sabra World / عالم صابرة
**Purpose:** Identity Protection, Anti-Drift and Rejection Rules

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines what must NOT happen when generating Sabra in images or video.

These constraints protect:

- Character identity
- Physical continuity
- Cultural authenticity
- Behavioral identity
- Wardrobe continuity
- Video continuity

A generation may be technically impressive and still be rejected if it violates these rules.

---

## 2. Primary Rule

**Never redesign Sabra.**

Every generation begins from the canonical Sabra identity.

The scene changes.

Sabra does not.

---

# FACE

## 3. Facial Identity Drift

Reject any generation where Sabra appears to be a different woman.

Do not change:

- Face shape
- Eye spacing
- Eye identity
- Nose structure
- Mouth proportions
- Jaw structure
- Forehead proportions
- Overall facial balance

Small changes caused naturally by expression or perspective are acceptable.

Structural redesign is not.

---

## 4. Facial Shape

Sabra has a soft rounded face.

Avoid:

- Sharp cheekbones
- Hollow cheeks
- Narrow face
- Long face
- Angular jaw
- Pointed chin
- Strong model-like bone structure
- Excessively sculpted facial features

Do not make Sabra thinner through facial reshaping.

---

## 5. Beauty Drift

Do not "improve" Sabra by converting her into a glamour model.

Avoid:

- Beauty-filter appearance
- Fashion-model facial proportions
- Artificial facial symmetry
- Excessive skin smoothing
- Cosmetic-advertising appearance
- Luxury beauty campaign styling

Sabra is attractive because she is Sabra.

---

# EYES

## 6. Eye Identity

Do not change Sabra's established wide expressive dark eyes.

Avoid:

- Narrow eyes
- Different eye shape
- Excessively enlarged artificial eyes
- Colored contact appearance
- Heavy false eyelashes
- Fashion eyeliner
- Smoky-eye makeup

Elegant black kohl remains canonical.

---

## 7. Kohl

Avoid:

- Excessively thick kohl
- Theatrical eye makeup
- Graphic eyeliner
- Glamour makeup
- Random changes in kohl design between continuous shots

Kohl enhances Sabra.

It does not become the subject of the image.

---

# AGE

## 8. Age Drift

Sabra is a relatively young grandmother.

Do not make her:

- Elderly
- Frail
- Extremely wrinkled
- Stooped
- Physically weak
- Visually decades older

Also do not make her:

- Very young
- Girl-like
- Fashion-model young
- Visually in her twenties

Preserve mature, active, healthy womanhood.

---

# SKIN

## 9. Skin Identity

Preserve Sabra's warm Egyptian wheat/olive-brown complexion.

Avoid:

- Artificially pale skin
- Major tanning changes
- Porcelain skin
- Plastic skin
- Wax-like texture
- Excessive retouching
- Heavy foundation appearance

Lighting may influence appearance without redefining skin identity.

---

# BODY

## 10. Body Drift

Sabra has a naturally full, healthy, feminine body.

Do not make her:

- Thin
- Skinny
- Fashion-model shaped
- Fitness-model shaped
- Extremely muscular
- Frail
- Artificially curvy
- Exaggerated in body size

Body proportions remain stable between shots.

---

## 11. Height and Scale

Canonical height:

**153 cm**

Avoid:

- Artificially long legs
- Elongated torso
- Model-like height
- Scale changes between shots
- Incorrect scale relative to furniture or other characters

Perspective may affect apparent size but not canonical proportions.

---

# HANDS

## 12. Hand Anatomy

Reject visibly malformed hands.

Avoid:

- Extra fingers
- Missing fingers
- Fused fingers
- Duplicated hands
- Impossible joints
- Broken-looking wrists
- Unnatural grip
- Objects passing through hands
- Incorrect hand-object contact

Hands are a high-priority production area.

---

## 13. Nails

Sabra has short, natural, clean, carefully maintained nails.

Never generate:

- Long nails
- Artificial nails
- Nail extensions
- Decorative nail art
- Heavy polish
- Glamour manicure styling

---

# HAIR AND HEADSCARF

## 14. Headscarf Drift

Sabra normally wears her canonical headscarf styling.

Avoid:

- Random scarf disappearance
- Random wrapping changes within a continuous scene
- High-fashion hijab styling
- Elaborate television styling
- Costume-like head wraps
- Physically impossible scarf behavior

Pattern and color may vary between wardrobes.

Identity language must remain coherent.

---

# JEWELRY

## 15. Earrings

Sabra's traditional Sa'idi gold earrings are a signature element.

Avoid:

- Earrings disappearing randomly
- Completely different earrings between continuous shots
- Modern fashion earrings
- Oversized theatrical costume jewelry
- Excessively luxurious jewelry

---

## 16. Gold

Gold must remain restrained.

Avoid:

- Excessive necklaces
- Heavy bracelet stacking
- Large numbers of rings
- Luxury-showcase styling
- Jewelry that dominates the character

---

# WARDROBE

## 17. Wardrobe Drift

Sabra may change clothing between appropriate scenes.

Within a continuous scene, avoid unexplained changes in:

- Garment
- Color
- Embroidery
- Headscarf
- Jewelry
- Footwear
- Sleeves
- Fabric pattern

Continuity matters more than decorative variation.

---

## 18. Cultural Styling

Avoid turning Sabra into a generic or theatrical "rural woman."

Do not use:

- Costume stereotypes
- Random folk costumes
- Unrelated regional clothing
- Fantasy Middle Eastern clothing
- Orientalist styling
- Tourist imagery
- Theatrical peasant imagery

Sabra's identity is specific, lived-in, and Egyptian Sa'idi.

---

# MAKEUP

## 19. Makeup Constraints

Avoid:

- Heavy foundation
- Contouring
- Strong lipstick
- Fashion makeup
- Glamour makeup
- Excessive blush
- False eyelashes
- Beauty influencer styling

Canonical makeup emphasis:

**Elegant black kohl.**

---

# BEHAVIOR

## 20. Presenter Drift

Sabra is not a generic television presenter.

Avoid:

- Permanent camera smile
- Constant direct eye contact with camera
- Artificial presentation gestures
- Holding every object toward camera
- Waiting for camera before acting
- Exaggerated enthusiasm
- Advertising-host behavior

The camera observes Sabra's world.

---

## 21. Personality Drift

Do not make Sabra:

- Timid
- Fragile
- Arrogant
- Aggressive by default
- Constantly angry
- Emotionless
- Childish
- Hyperactive
- Formal and distant
- Artificially cheerful

Preserve warmth plus authority.

---

## 22. Humor Drift

Avoid:

- Constant jokes
- Slapstick behavior
- Cartoon reactions
- Forced laughter
- Repeated identical laugh animations
- Turning Sabra into comic relief

Her humor comes from personality and timing.

---

# MOTION

## 23. Movement Drift

Avoid movement that makes Sabra appear:

- Elderly and fragile
- Like a fashion model
- Like a stage performer
- Hyperactive
- Mechanically animated
- Physically weightless

Movement should respect her body proportions and environment.

---

## 24. AI Gesture Repetition

Avoid repetitive generic AI gestures such as:

- Constant open-hand presentation
- Repeated pointing
- Identical nodding
- Repeated head tilting
- Constant hand waving
- Unnecessary arm movement while speaking

Gestures must have motivation.

---

# VIDEO

## 25. Facial Morphing

Reject video where Sabra's face changes during:

- Head turns
- Speaking
- Smiling
- Laughing
- Looking downward
- Looking upward
- Walking
- Camera movement

Sabra must remain the same woman frame to frame.

---

## 26. Body Morphing

Reject:

- Changing body size
- Changing height
- Changing limb proportions
- Clothing reshaping the body incorrectly
- Body geometry changing during movement

---

## 27. Age Morphing

Reject video where Sabra becomes visibly younger or older during motion.

Age appearance must remain stable.

---

## 28. Eye Morphing

Reject:

- Eye shape changes
- Eye size changes
- Eye-color changes
- Misaligned gaze
- Unnatural blinking
- Kohl changing during motion

---

## 29. Mouth and Lip-Sync

Reject:

- Facial identity distortion caused by speech
- Impossible mouth geometry
- Excessive lip movement
- Teeth changing between frames
- Lip-sync that changes Sabra's facial structure

Speech must adapt to Sabra's face.

Sabra's face must not adapt to the speech model.

---

## 30. Hands in Video

Pay special attention when Sabra:

- Picks something up
- Carries something
- Points
- Touches another person
- Handles tools
- Moves objects

Reject obvious anatomy or contact errors.

---

## 31. Clothing Mutation

Reject video where:

- Patterns move independently of fabric
- Embroidery changes
- Sleeves change length
- Garment color shifts unexpectedly
- Scarf changes design
- Jewelry appears or disappears

---

# ENVIRONMENT

## 32. Environment Must Not Redesign Sabra

Changing location must never automatically change Sabra's identity.

Field Sabra, home Sabra, family Sabra, and outdoor Sabra are the same woman.

Do not allow scene aesthetics to reinterpret the character.

---

## 33. Rural Stereotype Constraint

Avoid artificially exaggerating rural life.

Do not automatically make environments:

- Dirty
- Broken
- Primitive
- Extremely poor
- Historically outdated

Rural does not mean neglected.

Sabra's world should feel functional, cared for, authentic, and alive.

---

# PRODUCTION

## 34. Reference Conflict

When a generation conflicts with approved references:

**The reference wins.**

Do not modify the reference definition to justify a bad generation.

Regenerate the output.

---

## 35. Beauty Versus Identity

When choosing between:

A beautiful image with weak Sabra identity

and

A slightly less spectacular image with strong Sabra identity

choose:

**Strong Sabra identity.**

---

## 36. Continuity Versus Variety

Variety is valuable between scenes.

Continuity is mandatory within scenes.

Never introduce random visual changes merely to create variation.

---

## 37. Rejection Checklist

Reject when any major item is true:

- Different woman
- Wrong age
- Wrong face shape
- Wrong eyes
- Wrong kohl
- Wrong skin identity
- Thin body
- Wrong scale
- Incorrect scarf identity
- Missing signature earrings
- Excessive jewelry
- Long/artificial nails
- Malformed hands
- Presenter behavior
- Cultural caricature
- Facial morphing
- Body morphing
- Age morphing
- Wardrobe mutation
- Broken physical interaction
- Major continuity failure

---

## 38. Final Rule

**Never accept "close enough" when identity is wrong.**

Sabra's production value comes from the audience believing that the same woman exists across every image, shot, episode, environment, emotion, and generation system.

Protect the woman first.

Everything else is production.
