# Sabra World — Core Cast v1

Status: Concept definitions. Names, final visual identities, voices, and detailed character bibles are not canon until explicitly approved.

## 1. Sabra

**Status:** Master character / Canon.

The authentic Upper-Egyptian/rural Egyptian grandmother and mother figure at the center of Sabra World: wise, warm, strong, humorous, generous, and family-oriented.

See `SABRA_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` for current canon.

## 2. Grandson — Age 8–10

**Status:** Concept.

Lighthearted, bold, playful, and spoiled affectionately by his grandmother. His mischief is a recurring source of family comedy and interaction with Sabra.

## 3. Granddaughter — Age 11–13

**Status:** Concept.

Intelligent and curious. Her relationship with Sabra is built around questions, discussion, discovery, stories, and learning across generations.

## 4. Young Grandchild — Age 4–6

**Status:** Concept.

May be boy or girl until approved. Represents spontaneity, innocence, tenderness, and emotionally natural moments within the family scenes.

## Canon Boundary

The three grandchildren currently define dramatic functions only. Do not infer or permanently assign names, exact appearance, wardrobe, voice, gender for the youngest child, or additional personality traits until approved.
